# 🎧 Mini Bluetooth Pick-up 💿

A portable, retro-inspired music device, featuring a rotating decorative record, a moving tonearm, and a built-in speaker.

## The Idea

Connect a phone or laptop through Bluetooth and play music from Spotify. The sound comes from the pickup’s speaker while the record spins and the tonearm moves into position.

The device runs on a rechargeable battery. The paired phone or laptop supplies the music wirelessly.

**Status:** project concept — hardware selection and implementation are still being planned.

## Planned Features

-  Bluetooth music playback through a built-in speaker.
-  Rotating record and servo-controlled tonearm.
-  Physical controls for playback and volume.
-  LCD for menus and playback information.
-  RGB lighting and infrared remote control.
-  Rechargeable battery power.

Displaying song titles and synchronizing the mechanism with playback will depend on the Bluetooth module selected.

## Hardware Needed

**From the Arduino kit that I already own:**

- Arduino Mega 2560.
- Stepper motor with ULN2003 driver.
- Micro servo.
- LCD display.
- Joystick, buttons, and IR remote with receiver.
- RGB LED, photoresistor, potentiometer, resistors, and wires.

**Additional components:**

- Bluetooth audio receiver with an Arduino-compatible control interface.
- Audio amplifier, unless included in the receiver.
- Compact full-range speaker.
- Rechargeable power bank suitable for the system’s power requirements.
- Power connections and any required signal-level adaptation.
- Enclosure, decorative record, tonearm, and mounting materials.
